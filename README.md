## 🔥 What is Tempo Daddy?

Standard metronome practice for drum rudiments is broken. You spend half your time stopping the click, searching for a pen, logging your current BPM in a dusty notebook, and manually recalculating your next target. **LAME!** 🛑

**Tempo Daddy** takes the manipulative, addictive progression loops of modern idle games and turns them into a force for pure evil—err, *good!* 😈

It’s an intelligent, hyper-focused practice companion designed around one simple mission: **make setting new Personal Bests (PBs) as effortless and dopamine-packed as humanly possible.** You open the app, hit start, and let the gamified coaching engine blaze you through your routine. Your only job? **Set a new high score.** 💥🏆

---

## ⚡ The Secret Sauce: The Core Training Loop

No interruptions. No stopping to mess with dials. No manual logging. 

1. **Auto-Ramp Coaching:** For every exercise, Tempo Daddy looks at your current Personal Best (PB), starts you at **80% of your max**, and smoothly ramps up to **100% over 32 measures**. 📈
2. **One-Key Execution:** When you hit your max for the day (or break through a new ceiling), just slap **`ENTER`** on your keyboard (or click **DONE**). 
3. **Instant Progression:** Boom! 💥 Your new score is automatically saved, your streak jumps, and Tempo Daddy instantly launches you into your next exercise. 

No pen, no paper, no ruined flow state. Just pure practice velocity. 🚀

## 🎯 Key Features

### 🎛️ The Core Training Loop
* **Zero-Friction Practice:** Blaze through your entire routine without ever touching a notebook or resetting a metronome. 
* **Dynamic Control:** Want to take manual control of the tempo mid-session? You got it. Otherwise, let the 80% $\rightarrow$ 100% ramping algorithm push your physical limits for you.

### 📜 Intelligent Skill Queue & Sticking Engine
* **Preset & Custom Skills:** Choose from a built-in library of essential drum rudiments, add your own custom patterns, or hibernate skills you aren't actively training. 
* **Visual Sticking Patterns:** Clear, color-coded visual feedback so you never lose your place:
  * **Accents:** Highlighted in bold **YELLOW** (`R`, `L`, `K`) ⚡
  * **Unaccented:** Rendered in subtle **GREY** (`r`, `l`, `k`) ◽
* **Practice Notes:** Keep exercise-specific notes and reminders directly attached to each individual skill in your queue.

### 🎮 Gamified Dopamine Engine
* **High-Score Chasing:** Treat every practice session like an arcade game where you're hunting for new personal records. 🥇
* **Streak Counters & Progress Graphs:** Watch your speed gains over time with visual progress charts and daily streak tracking that keeps you coming back every single day. 📊
* **Constant Celebration:** Frequent, immediate visual feedback celebrating every micro-win to keep your brain hooked on improvement. 🎉✨

## 🎯 Technical Features

- **Precision Audio Engine**: High-accuracy Web Audio API scheduling for drift-free metronome playback with clean primary and secondary accents.
- **Subdivision & Meter Control**:
  - Support for quarter notes ($1/4$), eighth notes ($1/8$), sixteenth notes ($1/16$), and triplet feels across meters.
  - Interactive circular dial and manual BPM steppers with instant feedback.
- **Skill Library & Routine Builder**:
  - Organize exercises and rudiments across categories (Drumming, Piano, Guitar, General).
  - Built-in sticking notation displays (e.g., `R L R R L R L L` for paradiddles).
  - Target BPM goals, custom notes, and ability to hibernate or reorder skills.
- **Adaptive Tempo Ramping**:
  - Automatically ramp tempo over a set number of measures (e.g., starting at 80% of your Personal Best and ramping to 90% or beyond).
  - Eliminates manual adjustments during flow state practice.
- **Personal Bests & Progress Tracking**:
  - Automatically logs clean practice runs as new Personal Bests (PBs).
  - Sparklines and visual progress graphs showing tempo progression over time.
  - Practice streak calendars and routine completion summaries with celebration effects.
- **Offline & Local-First**:
  - Session history, skills, and stats persist locally in the browser so you can practice anywhere without an active internet connection.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites

- [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/) (v18 or higher recommended)
- `npm` (or `pnpm` / `yarn`)

### Installation & Local Development

1. **Clone the repository:**
   ```bash
   git clone git@github.com:TotalAardvark/TempoDaddy.git
   cd TempoDaddy
   ```

2. **Install dependencies:**
   ```bash
   npm install
   ```

3. **Start the development server:**
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```

4. **Build for production:**
   ```bash
   npm run build
   ```
