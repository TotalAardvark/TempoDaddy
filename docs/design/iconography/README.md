### Design Notes & User Feedback
- **Metronome Silhouette:** Shaded metallic metronome body with warm golden glowing pendulum ticker in mid-swing.
- **Golden Mustache ("Daddy"):** Prominent 3D gold-embossed handlebar mustache anchoring the lower base plate.
- **Background Finish:** Option B represents a lighter brushed cool gunmetal gradient with subtle horizontal linear grain. Lightened specifically to maximize contrast against the dark metronome casing without tinting noticeably blue.
- **Favicon Strategy:** At micro resolutions (`16px`, `32px`), full metronome bodies become illegible. The golden mustache alone on a dark squircle retains instant silhouette clarity and brand punch.
